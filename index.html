01About

Finance by day, building with AI the rest of the time.

First year at South China Normal University — an island campus in Panyu, Guangzhou. A long way to go, and that’s rather the point.

I’m a finance undergraduate who got curious about what one person can build with AI — and never quite stopped. Most of what I know came from opening an editor, breaking something, and trying again.

Alongside coursework I’m a content creator, documenting what learning this actually looks like from zero: the tools, the dead ends, the small wins. I also serve as an operations officer at the Digital Intelligence Business Association, where I help run events and keep projects moving.
